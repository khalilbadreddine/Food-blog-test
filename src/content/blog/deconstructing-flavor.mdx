Food photography is aesthetic, but it rarely teaches you **how** or **why** a dish works structurally.

When you look at a photograph of a burger, a layered cake, or a bowl of ramen, key physical questions remain hidden:
- What prevents moisture from softening the bottom crust?
- Which order of components maximizes structural friction so ingredients don't slide out?
- How does thermal transfer melt cheese without overcooking underlying greens?

## The 3D Exploded View Advantage

By translating recipes into low-poly 3D exploded views and diagrammatic models, we unlock immediate clarity:

1. **Exploded Assembly:** Sliders allow you to pull components apart, observing layer thickness and sequence.
2. **Component Isolation:** See individual sub-assemblies before they combine into the final dish.
3. **Reproducible Consistency:** Understand physical principles like steam vents, emulsion stability, and starch hydration.
