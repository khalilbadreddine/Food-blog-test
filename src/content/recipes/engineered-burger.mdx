## Structural Integrity Analysis

When designing a burger system, **layer order dictates physical stability and mouthfeel**.

Place the fat/emulsion barrier (sauce) directly on the toasted bottom bun to repel vegetable juices. The melted American cheese acts as a structural adhesive binding the patty and pickles together during consumption.
