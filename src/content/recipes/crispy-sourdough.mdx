## Fermentation Thermal Dynamics

Hydration level (75%) directly impacts steam expansion during oven spring. By trapping steam inside the sealed Dutch oven during the initial 20 minutes of baking, starch on the exterior gelatinizes into an ultra-thin, golden blistered crust.
